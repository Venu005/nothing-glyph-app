Sand timer: which look?

The sand is live physics. Use Flip to turn the hourglass over like a real one, which sends the time left back the other way. On its side pauses it, and the slider tilts it to any angle. The left matrix is the Phone (3) at 25×25. The right is the 13×13 (the 4a Pro shows it as a still, redrawn each minute). Click every style you like.
