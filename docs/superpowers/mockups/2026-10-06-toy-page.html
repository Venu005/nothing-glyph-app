Toy page (opens from the grid, swipe between toys)

Tap a card on the home grid and you land here. Swipe left or right for the next toy, and tap ← to go back to the grid. Every toy page has the same shape. The bottom button turns into TURN ON IN GLYPH TOYS (with the red dot) when the toy isn't switched on yet; tapping it shows the two-step setup. The button now sits in its own bottom bar, and the page scrolls above it, so the last row never goes under the button.
